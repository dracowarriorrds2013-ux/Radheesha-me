02 / School

St. Anne’s College, Kurunegala

Founded 1867

Started as the Roman Catholic English School in Kurunegala, with 28 boys and 19 girls and two teachers. Renamed St. Anne’s Boys College in 1881, and in 1934 the De La Salle Brothers took over and bought the present 12-acre land, bringing discipline and quality education. Brother Theodoret of Mary set up a laboratory and started science studies in 1943.
